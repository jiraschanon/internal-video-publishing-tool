Privacy Policy

This application uses TikTok OAuth to allow users to upload and publish videos to their own TikTok accounts.

The application only accesses data explicitly authorized by the user during the OAuth process.

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data with third parties.

Uploaded content is transmitted directly to TikTok’s servers using official APIs and is not stored permanently by this application.

This application is intended for internal use only.
